# Photo Showcase

A photography portfolio and album-delivery site for presenting the photographer's work and letting visitors obtain shareable photos.

## Language

**Album**:
A curated group of photos presented together, such as an event, trip, or themed series.
_Avoid_: Folder, gallery

**Public album**:
An album intended to be discoverable through the site and suitable for portfolio presentation.

**Unlisted album**:
An album omitted from public discovery but accessible to anyone who has its link.
_Avoid_: Private album, protected album

**Published album**:
An album explicitly enabled by the photographer for the site; albums are unpublished by default.
_Avoid_: Detected album, available folder

**Photo**:
A Lightroom-processed JPG selected for publication in an album; RAW source files are never photos in this product.
_Avoid_: Asset, image file

**Display JPG**:
A resized and optimized version of a photo used for browsing and social sharing.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, social photo

**Original JPG**:
The full-size JPG exported from Lightroom and retained as the highest-quality downloadable version.
_Avoid_: Original, RAW

**Album archive**:
A ZIP download containing an album's Original JPGs.
_Avoid_: Album download, folder download

**Album date**:
The photographer-specified calendar date used to label and order an album; it is not inferred from photo metadata or file timestamps.
_Avoid_: Upload date, EXIF date
